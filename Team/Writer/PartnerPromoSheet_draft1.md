# Aging Transitions Workshop
## A Planning Partnership Invitation

**Clark Johnson, Lopez Island**

---

### What This Is

Most adults have — or know they should have — a will, a power of attorney, and a healthcare directive. Those documents matter. But they leave a wide range of practical, relational, and logistical challenges entirely unaddressed.

Who can your friends and family call when you're hospitalized and they're getting no information? Who knows where your important documents are, or what bills need to be paid, if you're suddenly incapacitated? When daily functioning starts to slip, who recognizes it — and who knows what to do?

The **Aging Transitions Workshop** is a full-day Saturday event on Lopez Island for adults 65 and older, built around three specific gap areas:

1. **Information access.** HIPAA rules mean that friends and family — even close ones — often cannot get basic health status information from providers or hospitals. This leaves people who want to help unable to coordinate effectively. There are straightforward steps that can change this, and most people don't know about them.

2. **Sudden incapacity.** A fall, a stroke, a medical crisis: who knows who to call? Who has authority to make decisions and handle immediate practical needs? If there is no clear answer, family and neighbors end up improvising under stress.

3. **Slow decline.** Gradual changes in health or functioning are harder to recognize and respond to than sudden ones. When is it time to intervene? What resources are available? What does it mean to be a caregiver, and what support exists for caregivers on Lopez Island?

The workshop format combines presentations on each area, breakout discussions, and hands-on activity sessions. Every participant leaves with an individualized action plan and a Lopez Island resource guide.

Specifically, I'm inviting partners to:

- **Help define what the workshop needs to accomplish** — contributing to the framing of objectives based on what you're seeing in the community
- **Advise on content** — reviewing presentations in your area of expertise and flagging what's missing, overstated, or wrong for this audience
- **Engage with participants during the workshop itself** — for example, facilitating or participating in breakout group discussions

This is a community effort. The organizations I'm approaching have direct experience with the people this workshop is meant to serve. That experience belongs in the room.
