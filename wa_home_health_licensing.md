# Washington State Home Health Care Licensing Requirements

> **Source:** Washington State Department of Health (DOH) — WAC 246-335
> **Last Updated:** June 2026

---

## Overview: Two License Types

Washington State distinguishes between two types of in-home services agencies, both licensed by the DOH under **WAC 246-335**:

| License Type | Services Provided |
|---|---|
| **Home Health Agency** | Skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy — medical services |
| **Home Care Agency** | Non-medical services: bathing, dressing, ambulation, meal prep, activities of daily living |

---

## Home Care Agency — License Requirements

Home care agencies provide **non-medical** support services to ill, disabled, or vulnerable people with functional limitations.

### Required Application Documents

1. **Orientation Class Certificate** — Copy of "certificate of completion" from the In-Home Services Orientation Class. *Applications will not be processed without this.*
2. **Completed Application** — In-home services application form plus the initial licensing fee.
3. **General Liability Insurance** — Proof of current commercial general liability insurance (per WAC 246-335-320(2)(b)).
4. **Disclosure Statements** — For the on-site administrator and supervisor of direct care services; must be dated within **three months** of the application date (per WAC 246-335-320(2)(d)).
5. **Criminal History Background Checks** — Processed through the Washington State Patrol for the administrator and direct care supervisor; dated within three months of application.
6. **Government-Issued Business Licenses** — Copies for each office location (may include state, county, or city licenses).
7. **Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) Worksheet** — Completed worksheet.
8. **Organizational Structure Description** — Listing names of officers, administrator, direct care supervisor, and other key positions.
9. **Service Area Management Description** — Explaining how the agency will manage and supervise services across all requested service areas.
10. **Services Description** — Detailing which services the agency will provide directly and which will be offered under contract.

## Home Health Agency — License Requirements

Home health agencies provide **skilled medical services** including nursing and therapy to help clients recover from illness or surgery, or maintain their highest level of health and independence.

### Required Application Documents

The requirements are the same as for Home Care Agencies, with these key differences:

- The clinical lead is titled **Director of Clinical Services** (rather than Supervisor of Direct Care Services)
- Disclosure statements and background checks are required for both the **on-site administrator** and the **director of clinical services**

All other documents (orientation certificate, application, insurance, business licenses, FTE worksheet, org structure, service descriptions) are identical to home care agency requirements.

---

## Application Process

1. Complete the In-Home Services Orientation Class and obtain your certificate.
2. Gather and submit all required documents to the address shown in the application packet.
3. DOH reviews materials; the **Office of Investigation and Inspection** contacts you to schedule an initial on-site survey.
4. Pass the survey to receive your license.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Per WAC 246-335-320(4), DOH may close out an application **with no refund** if all required steps are not completed within **nine months**.

---

## Medicare Certification (Home Health Agencies Only)

State licensure is a **prerequisite** for federal Medicare certification. Additional steps include:

1. Review federal Medicare regulations (42 CFR 484 Parts A–E; OASIS regulations).
2. Contact DOH's **Certificate of Need (CN) Program** before applying (email: fslcon@doh.wa.gov).
3. Once CN approval is granted, submit **Medicare Form 855A** to the CMS Fiscal Intermediary.
4. Submit completed application materials to DOH's Medicare Program.
5. Pass a separate **Medicare on-site survey** conducted by the Office of Investigation and Inspection.

---

## Worker-Level Requirements

- **Home Care Aides** must be certified and complete required training per **Initiative 1163** (effective January 7, 2012).
- Certification must be obtained within **200 days of hire**.
- Training, background checks, and certification are overseen by the WA Department of Health.

---

## Medicaid / DSHS Contracting

Some home care agencies contract with the **Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS)** to serve Medicaid-eligible clients. These agencies are surveyed by **Area Agency on Aging (AAA)** staff.

- **AAA Contact:** 1-800-562-3263
- **Division of Developmental Disabilities:** 1-800-562-3022
